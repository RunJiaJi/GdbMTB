GdbMTB

With the increasing research interets on magnetotactic bacteria diversity, the genome data of MTB continues to grow.

In order to promote data sharing and facilitate comprehensive analysis, we have established the Genomic database of Magnetotactic Bacteria, GdbMTB.

GdbMTB includes three parts: key (primary) data, analysis (secondary) data, and technical support (analysis tools and methods).

The key data include publicly published magnetotactic bacteria genome data, sampling metadata, magnetosome gene data, etc. The analysis data includes basic analysis information such as the quality, phylogenetic classification, and living environment of the MTB. Technical support provides related methods, tools and processes for the genomics analysis of MTB (such as MagCluster).
